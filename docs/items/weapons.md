## Stat Glossary
- **Damage**: Min–Max base damage range.
- **Accuracy**: Hit chance modifier.
- **Bonuses**: Extra stats (HP/TP, Power, Defense, Aura, Dexterity, etc.).
- **Eon Value**: Vendor/valuation hint (if shown on source).
