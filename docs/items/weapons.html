Weapons

This page lists weapons in Endless Online Recharged, with quick stats for Damage, Accuracy, and notable bonuses. We’ll add locations, crafting, and drop info next.

Tip: Use your browser’s find (Ctrl/⌘+F) to jump to a weapon.

Stat Glossary

  • Damage: Min–Max base damage range.
  • Accuracy: Hit chance modifier.
  • Bonuses: Extra stats (HP/TP, Power, Defense, Aura, Dexterity, etc.).
  • Eon Value: Vendor/valuation hint (if shown on source).
